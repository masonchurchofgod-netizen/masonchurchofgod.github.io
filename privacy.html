Privacy Policy: Sunday Launcher

Mason Church of God, 204 Williams Street, Mason, OH 45040. Last updated September 30, 2026.

What Sunday Launcher is

Sunday Launcher is a private tool that Mason Church of God uses to run its own Sunday livestream. It runs on a single computer in the church building and is used only by the church's media team. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside the church signs in to it.

Use of YouTube API Services

Sunday Launcher uses YouTube API Services. By watching or chatting on the church's YouTube channel you are also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

The church signs in to Sunday Launcher with the church's own Google account only. With that permission, Sunday Launcher:

  • schedules the church's livestream broadcasts and sets their title, description, and settings;
  • reads back those settings and the stream's health to check that the broadcast is working;
  • reads the live chat on the church's own broadcasts, and posts welcome messages and short announcements there, but only after a person at the church approves each message.

Information it handles

  • Public chat and comments: the public display name, channel or profile ID, and message text of people who chat on the church's YouTube livestream or comment on the church's Facebook post. This is used to greet people by name and to spot spam.
  • A small welcome list: the church may keep a first name to use in greetings for people it knows. People are only greeted by real first name when the pastor has approved it and the person is marked as OK to greet. Anyone can ask not to be greeted or tracked, and the church can delete a person's record at any time; once deleted they are not added again.
  • Sign-in tokens: the church's Google sign-in token is stored in the computer's secure keychain. It is never shared.

Where it is kept and who sees it

  • Everything is stored on the church's computer and in the church's encrypted backups. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.
  • To write a greeting or sort comments, the text of the messages and only the first names or display names needed for that greeting are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service, which the church uses to draft text. Nothing is posted until a person at the church approves it.
  • Records are kept for as long as the church needs them for its services; backups are kept for 30 days.

Removing access

The church can remove Sunday Launcher's access to its Google account at any time from the Google account permissions page (Security > Third-party apps with account access). The church's Google account is the only account Sunday Launcher ever uses.

Contact

Questions or requests (for example, to not be greeted, or to have your record removed): email masonchurchofgod@gmail.com, or speak to someone at Mason Church of God.
